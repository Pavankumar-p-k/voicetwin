I

It pushes back

"I built a React application." "What part of that did you personally build? Give me a number." Vague answers raise the pressure; evidence calms it.

II

Evidence, not vibes

Every answer is scored against a pre-written five-point rubric by inspectable detectors — no LLM opinion anywhere in the score. Every checkmark is defensible.

III

Coaching that measures

Weakest question → targeted practice → before 2/5 → after 5/5. The deliverable is not a score. It is the delta.

"We are not asking a model whether the answer sounds impressive. We are checking whether the evidence the question requires is actually there."
